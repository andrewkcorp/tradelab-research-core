# Community expectations

Be respectful, constructive and specific. Critique claims, assumptions and code,
not people. Welcome questions and acknowledge uncertainty. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing, personal attacks and posting private information
are not acceptable in issues, pull requests or other project spaces.

The maintainer may ask for changes, remove content, close discussions or restrict
participation to protect contributors. Decisions should be proportional and, when
appropriate, explained without exposing private reports.

For a sensitive report, use the repository's private reporting channel if available.
If no private channel is available, ask the maintainer to establish one without
posting sensitive details publicly. Security reports follow SECURITY.md.

No contributor is required to disclose personal circumstances, account details,
trading performance or proprietary data to participate.
